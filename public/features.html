Features

Everything in one calm place

Three ways to see your work, the details that keep it clear, and a search that finds anything.

List view

Every task, one clean table

The fastest way to plan. Type a task, press Enter, and the box is ready for the next one.

  • Priority and due date columns
  • Overdue tasks stand out on their own
  • Completed tasks move to the bottom
Board view

Move work forward, card by card

Drag tasks between columns to show where they stand. Columns are yours to shape.

  • Drag and drop with a mouse or a finger
  • Add, rename and delete columns
  • Deleting a column never deletes its tasks
Calendar view

Deadlines you can actually see

Each task sits on its due date. Plan a month at a glance and reschedule by dragging.

  • Add a task straight onto any day
  • Drag a task to a new date
  • On phones, tap a day to see its tasks
Task details

Subtasks, tags and priority

Open any task to add the details that make it clear: smaller steps, labels and how urgent it is.

  • Subtasks with a live progress count
  • Create tags as you type
  • High, Medium and Low priority

Search and filters

Find anything in a second

Search names and descriptions, then narrow down by tag, priority or due date. Works in every view.

  • Press / anywhere to start searching
  • Overdue, due today and next 7 days
  • Matches are highlighted as you type

Sign in your way, safely

Use Continue with Google, or an email and password. Your work stays private to your account.

  • Google sign-in, checked on the server
  • Passwords hashed, never stored as text
  • You stay logged in for 7 days
