See your work the way you think

Switch between a list, a board and a calendar in one click. Same tasks, three angles.

List

A clean table of every task with its priority and due date. Type a name, press Enter, keep going.

Board

Drag cards between columns like To do, Doing and Done. Add, rename or remove columns any time.

Calendar

Every task on its due date. Drag a task to another day to reschedule it in a second.
